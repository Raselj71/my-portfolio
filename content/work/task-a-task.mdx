## Problem

Teams that hand work back and forth, such as field and service crews, agencies, and small businesses, were running tasks in one app, conversations in another, and costs in a spreadsheet. Commitments disappeared between tools.

## Role

Lead developer of the mobile app since its first commit in July 2025. I set up the Expo and React Native codebase, the multi-environment EAS pipeline, and the testing setup, and I own the calling feature end to end.

## Technical decisions

<Stack items={["Expo", "React Native", "Twilio Video", "CallKit", "PushKit", "ConnectionService", "Firebase Cloud Messaging"]} />

- **Calls that behave like phone calls.** One-to-one and group audio and video on Twilio Video, with CallKit and PushKit on iOS and ConnectionService with a foreground service on Android. Calls ring on the lock screen and from a cold start, and video continues in picture-in-picture.
- **Native modules where JavaScript is not enough.** Custom VoIP push and lock-screen modules, plus Expo config plugins for the native pieces.
- **One codebase, three platforms.** The same calling and chat code runs in the Android, iOS, and web builds.
